EcoSentia

Lexical support screening for bio-inspired design claims

This tool measures how far a claim's vocabulary co-occurs with the vocabulary of published abstracts. It does not assess whether a claim is true. Every result requires reading of the underlying sources before it is used.

State the claim to be screened

What makes a screenable claim

A screenable claim names a biological mechanism and an asserted outcome. Without both, the query is either too general to be informative or too narrow for any abstract to satisfy — and both failures look identical to an absence of evidence.

  • Screenable Lotus leaf micro-papillae with wax crystals produce self-cleaning surfaces that reduce facade maintenance water use.
  • Too general Nature-inspired materials are more sustainable.
  • Two claims Spider silk is stronger than steel and can be produced industrially at low cost. Screen each separately.
